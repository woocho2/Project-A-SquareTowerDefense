Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one polished presentation image of a compact, unified top HUD for a Korean cute cube tower defense game. This is a visual concept, not an implementation diagram. Landscape 1536x1024 composition, razor-sharp clean 2D cartoon game UI, readable Korean typography.

DESIGN LANGUAGE: light creamy ivory surfaces, warm tan/golden-brown thin double outlines and bold corner ornaments shaped like rounded right-angle brackets, matching decorated square tower tiles. Restrained soft pastel backgrounds paired with saturated red, rich yellow gold, and sky blue resource icons. Flat cel shading with subtle warm gradients, dark chocolate-brown text. Clean and friendly collectible-card-game styling. NOT photoreal wood, not parchment, not ornate medieval filigree, no pixel art, no heavy glow. Large clear numerals, compact icon silhouettes. No characters or portraits in the HUD.

MAIN SUBJECT: a SINGLE horizontally unified header with three well-proportioned internal zones and shared outer frame, plenty of breathing room. Left 20%: red cartoon heart with bold dark-brown outline, large value “20”, short label “체력”, and small secondary label “적 한도” beneath. Center 48%: small uppercase stage label “STAGE 01” next to the large Korean stage name “아스가르드”; beneath this show “웨이브 07 / 40” with 07 emphasized; on the lowest thin line show “적 턴 03 / 10” and exactly ten small square progress ticks, three filled warm amber and seven unfilled ivory/tan, to convey turns within the wave, separate from wave number. Right 32%: two clearly separate resources, a simple yellow gold coin embossed with a three-point crown followed by “1,250” and the short label “골드”; a saturated cyan-blue faceted diamond followed by “35” and the short label “젬”. Numbers are stronger than small labels. Use thin interior separators, no excessive individual boxes, no plus purchase buttons, no gear or other unsolicited controls. All five requested facts must be read at once; wave and turn progress must not be confused. Exact visible labels are the quoted Korean and numbers above. No extra English headings or invented sentences.

PRESENTATION: show an enlarged, crisp, isolated version of this exact header on the upper 40% of a quiet pale warm-gray design board, with a small understated caption above reading “상단 통합 HUD”. In the lower 55% show the SAME layout at an appropriately compact in-game scale near the top edge of a wide landscape game viewport mockup, occupying only around 12-15% of that viewport height. Below that HUD the game board is a muted, simplified top-down square tiled arena: ivory tower tiles with tan corner brackets, a dark navy path loop, a handful of tiny cute square cube enemies and small tower tokens. Keep the board low contrast and deliberately secondary so the UI is the focus. No additional panels or buttons in the viewport. Maintain exact icon colors, spacing and data between enlarged view and viewport. No arrows, no callouts, no annotation boxes, no watermark. Sophisticated legible compact UI, not a mobile-game ad.
