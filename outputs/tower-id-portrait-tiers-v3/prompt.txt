Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset: one FIVE-TIER EVOLUTION comparison concept sheet for a Norse SD tower-defense game.
Input: supplied sheet of the same dark-haired gold-half-mask character in five square ID-photo portraits.

User wants a STRONG VISUAL FEELING OF GROWTH across tiers. Design ONE coherent frame family evolving from humble to legendary, not five unrelated frame motifs. Each next frame must visibly inherit ALL the defining structures of the previous frame and add an obvious new stage of craftsmanship. The outline language, wing/crest shapes and placement remain consistent across the whole series.

Preserve the supplied character portraits, scale, upright frontal pose, facial expression, costume colors and pale tier-colored backgrounds. Frame and tier background are the only tier-dependent art. Character must not evolve or change clothes.

Five evolution stages, left to right / top to bottom:
1 BRONZE: a plain sturdy copper rounded-square metal frame, single border band, four small simple corner caps. Only subtle tiny V-shaped notches hint at the future wing motif. NO gem, wings, crest, runic glow or fancy engravings. This should look clearly like the starting/base frame.
2 SILVER: same exact underlying structure in cool silver; corner caps are upgraded into a modest raised paired-chevron/wing-root shape. A second thin inner metal trim becomes visible. Keep it distinctly modest, NO big top crest or gemstone.
3 GOLD: same frame in bright gold, same corner wing roots extend into SMALL clean two-feather wing plates. A SMALL plain crest plate appears at top center. No gigantic gem. First obvious heroic step, visibly builds on SILVER.
4 MITHRIL: same underlying frame in luminous turquoise metal; SAME wing plates become larger THREE-feather wings with clearly layered depth. Top-center crest grows and receives ONE turquoise gemstone, a subtle thin glowing trim along the inner edge. A clearly stronger, magically awakened version of GOLD.
5 DIAMOND: the COMPLETE FINAL EVOLUTION of this exact same design: pearly white and icy pale-blue metal, SAME corner wings now FOUR clear crystalline feather segments, SAME central crest becomes a brilliant faceted diamond with a compact mirrored wing crown. Double border trim with restrained cyan/lavender iridescence. Looks like the finished legendary version of the Bronze foundation, much more prestigious than MITHRIL.

Visual progression must be immediately obvious even in grayscale: plain border -> upgraded caps/double trim -> small wings/crest -> larger layered wings/gem -> crystalline perfected wing crown. Common broad V/wing shape repeated on all stages. DO NOT switch between studs, unrelated knotwork, flowers, leaves, random armor or crystals as independent tier themes.

GEOMETRY: all portrait openings are IDENTICAL rounded squares, identical size and center, equal visible character area. All tier frames fit the SAME allocated square tile footprint, front-facing with no perspective. Growing ornamentation occupies the shared outer frame band and corner zones; never intrudes over the face, masks the hair or narrows the opening. Use compact clear feather silhouettes rather than large protruding wings. Make the advancing tiers feel visibly more complete rather than just differently colored.

STYLE: friendly chunky SD game art, clean bold shapes, 2-3 broad shading values. Do not add dense miniature filigree, busy runes, floating sparkles, realistic metal scratches or large glows. Readable at small board-tile scale.
Sheet layout stays three tiles across the top BRONZE / SILVER / GOLD, two centered below MITHRIL / DIAMOND, same neutral warm off-white sheet background, same small Latin tier labels. One cohesive five-stage progression shown as one polished comparison sheet.
