Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE finished tower information card UI concept for a cozy 2D Norse fantasy tower-defense mobile game.
Reference images: Image 1, the sports collectible player card, is ONLY a reference for its portrait-first hierarchy, narrow portrait silhouette, compact identity rail on the upper left, centered name and two-column numerical stat section; do not copy its player, logos, brand, badges, typography or dark colors. Image 2 is the game's 2x2 Sword tower character atlas. Use ONLY its TOP-LEFT red Hero knight face as the character identity and cartoon art reference. Do not show the other three characters or the atlas grid.

ONE card, large and fully visible, upright front view. Card width:height about 9:17. Thin plain warm-neutral background around it. No phone, scene, board, perspective, or alternate card. The card should read as an actual mobile game tower info panel, not a poster.
Outer silhouette: collectible fantasy card with broad softly rounded upper shoulders, subtle rounded crest notch at the top center, and gently tapering lower corners meeting a small rounded bottom point. Outer bronze metal tier frame, warm copper-bronze rather than saturated orange, restrained gold inner pinstripe and small tasteful Norse knot/rune accents at corners. Smooth corners, no harsh octagonal buttons. Interior warm ivory cream / pale beige gradient, slight glossy upper highlight and gentle inner shadow. Friendly bright cartoon UI. Text dark brown, clean bold legible type. The character's red/silver equipment should remain its existing colors; bronze color indicates CARD TIER through the outer rim and bronze gem, not a recolor of the face.

Layout:
UPPER PORTRAIT section about the upper 40% of the card.
- Upper left: bold short "Lv 1" and tiny "BRONZE" text.
- Beneath Lv 1, a narrow vertical column of FOUR small square rounded icon badges: bronze tier gem, solid red attack-type swatch, sword emblem, diagonal slash attribute. No icon labels. Compact and evenly spaced.
- To the right of this rail, a LARGE existing red Hero knight FACE-ONLY character, approximately square cheeks with friendly eyes and smile, brown bangs, red/silver helmet with white sword crest and small red/gold decorative flares. Keep the identity, friendly expression and three-tone clean cartoon look of the atlas top-left. No shoulders, body, hands, floating weapons, circular avatar crop, or extra frame around the face. Its helmet and red flares can slightly overlap the INNER portrait panel border, but stay within the main card.
- Subtle pale cream portrait backdrop; no scenery or large busy pattern. The face is the main focus and occupies most of this portrait section.

Below portrait: centered bold tower name "HERO". Small gold divider beneath, and a compact cream target strip reading "Target: First" with a small dropdown arrow at right.

LOWER STAT SECTION: EXACTLY SIX basic stats in THREE ROWS and TWO COLUMNS. FIFA-like clean paired stat columns but friendly cream UI.
Each stat entry: small consistent icon, SHORT label, a much bigger bold dark numeric value. Numeric values most prominent. Thin gold vertical divider between the two columns, very subtle row rules. Roomy padding so labels and numbers do not touch.
Row 1 LEFT sword icon "PWR" value "15"; RIGHT double arrows icon "HITS" value "2".
Row 2 LEFT crosshair ring icon "RNG" value "2"; RIGHT target reticle icon "CRIT" value "25%".
Row 3 LEFT clock icon "ACT" value "2/3"; RIGHT burst star icon "CDMG" value "200%".
Exactly 3 rows, no additional stat cells. All six active and clearly legible.

BOTTOM UI:
- Two equal rounded slim SKILL buttons side by side, with small gold sword/rune icons and short labels "SKILL 1" and "SKILL 2". Small subtle dropdown/detail chevron on each. No extra multiplier/duration/mastery/stack grid; this card is a collapsed skill state.
- A small footer line "SELL" on the left and a bright blue gem icon plus bold "100" on the right, above the rounded bottom point.
Maintain equal side margins, precise spacing, minimal decoration and comfortable tap targets. Never include sports branding, human athlete imagery, Korean long text, extra explanatory captions outside the card, watermark, dark backgrounds inside the card or a third stat column.
